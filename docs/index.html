Markets research

A place to work through ideas about markets and what things are worth.

I'm curious about why prices move the way they do, and what happens when you test an idea about a market instead of just trusting your first instinct on it. This site is where that curiosity turns into actual things — models, tools, backtests — mostly built around oil and macro markets. It's a work in progress and will keep changing as I keep poking at it.

About

I like figuring out what something is actually worth versus what people assume it's worth, and building small tools to check that gap instead of just going with a hunch. Most of what ends up here comes out of that — oil and macro markets mostly, how commodity prices work their way into a company's numbers, and whether a rule for picking cheap stocks actually holds up once you go back and check it properly.
